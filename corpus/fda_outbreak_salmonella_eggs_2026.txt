Title: Outbreak Investigation of Salmonella: Eggs (July 2026)

Status: The investigation concluded with the CDC declaring the outbreak over and FDA
closing its investigation as of September 3, 2026.

Key Dates:
- Illness onset range: November 21, 2025 to August 12, 2026
- Recall initiated: July 22, 2026
- Final update: September 3, 2026

Case Summary: Total illnesses: 134 across 18 states (AR, AZ, CA, CO, GA, IL, LA, MI, MO, MS,
NC, NM, NV, NY, OK, SC, TX, WV). Hospitalizations: 34. Deaths: 0. 85% of 54 ill persons with
available information reported eating eggs before illness.

Recalled Products: Midwest Poultry Services, L.P. recalled white shell eggs and brown
cage-free eggs produced in Texas between June 6-July 3, 2026. Affected brands included Kroger,
Simple Truth, Brookshire's, Country Morning, and Sunups, with specific identifying code
P-1950 or 0840962 and Julian dates 157-184.

Investigation Findings: Epidemiological, laboratory, and traceback evidence determined that
shell eggs produced by Midwest Poultry Services, L.P. in Texas are a likely source of
illnesses. Three environmental samples from FDA inspection matched the outbreak strain via
whole genome sequencing.

Consumer Recommendations: Discard recalled eggs, clean surfaces thoroughly, and wash hands,
utensils, and surfaces with hot, soapy water before and after handling raw eggs.
