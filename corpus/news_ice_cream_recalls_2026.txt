Title: Ice Cream Recall Details (The Healthy)

Companies/Brands:
- Galliker Dairy Co., Inc. (Altoona, PA) -- DQ Mix Choc product
- Wells Enterprises/Wells Dairy (Iowa) -- Blue Ribbon Classics brand

Products Recalled:
- DQ Mix Choc in five-gallon bags (74 cases) -- chocolate blend for soft-serve ice cream
  machines
- Blue Ribbon Classics French vanilla ice cream in one-gallon clear pails (195 cases)

States Affected:
- Galliker product: Delaware, Maryland, New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania
- Wells product: Wisconsin (Kenosha area)

Reasons for Recall:
- Galliker: foreign material found in product (identified as metal shavings)
- Wells: elevated coliforms (bacteria indicating potential exposure to unsanitary conditions)

Risk Level: Both recalls assigned Class II status, meaning the products may cause temporary
or medically reversible adverse health consequences or where the probability of serious
adverse health consequences is remote.

Recall Dates: Galliker recall initiated May 21, 2026. Wells recall initiated June 10, 2026.
