Title: Outbreak Investigation of E. coli O145:H28: Frozen Blueberries (July 2026)

Status: Ongoing investigation as of September 11, 2026.

Case Summary: Total Illnesses: 17. Hospitalizations: 6. Deaths: 0. Illness Onset Dates:
May 11, 2026 to August 6, 2026. States Affected: Florida and Georgia (confirmed cases).

Source & Products Recalled: The outbreak is linked to frozen blueberries from Frutas y
Hortalizas del Sur S.A., San Carlos, Chile.

Recalled Products:
1. GreenWise Organic Whole Blueberries (10-oz and 48-oz packages)
2. GreenWise Organic Whole Mixed Berries (10-oz and 48-oz packages)
3. Great Value Organic Triple Berry Blend (10-oz, Lot Code 6040 01-6, Best by Date
   February 9, 2028)
All recalled products feature lot code 60401 (printed as 6 040 01) or 6040 01-6 with Best by
Date of February 9, 2028.

Distribution: Products distributed to 26 states: Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia,
Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi,
Missouri, Nebraska, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, South Carolina, South
Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, West Virginia, Wisconsin, plus Puerto Rico.

Key Recommendations: The FDA advises not to eat, sell, or serve recalled products. Consumers
should check freezers, discard contaminated items or return them, and clean surfaces that
contacted recalled berries. Those experiencing symptoms should consult healthcare providers
immediately.
