Title: Outbreak Investigation of Infant Botulism: Powdered Infant Formula (June 2026)

Key Dates: Investigation Period June-August 2026. Illness Onset April-May 2026. Last
Illness Onset May 31, 2026. Recall Date June 13, 2026. Outbreak Declared Over August 27, 2026.

Summary: The FDA and CDC investigated a multistate outbreak of infant botulism linked to Nara
Organics Whole Milk Organic Powdered Infant Formula. Investigators determined the
contamination source was shared with a previous 2025 ByHeart formula outbreak, both products
using milk from the same suppliers.

Product Recalled: Nara Organics Whole Milk Organic Powdered Infant Formula. Recalled products
should no longer be available for sale. Recalled product has Best By dates ending in 2027.
The product was distributed through Target retail stores, Target.com, and Nara.com between
July 2025 and June 2026.

Case Counts: Total Illnesses 4 confirmed cases. Hospitalizations 4. Deaths 0. States Affected:
California, Pennsylvania, Washington.

Investigation Findings: Genetic analysis revealed one clinical sample from an infant in this
outbreak is a genetic match to a sample from the 2025 ByHeart outbreak. Both formulas used
organic whole milk supplied by Organic West Milk and spray-dried by Dairy Farms of America,
indicating a common source of contamination between the two outbreaks.

Consumer and Retailer Recommendations: Check homes for recalled product and discard it. Wash
items and surfaces (like bottles, counters, or formula dispensers) that may have touched the
formula using hot soapy water. Seek immediate care if infants show symptoms like poor
feeding, loss of head control, or difficulty swallowing. Healthcare providers should contact
the Infant Botulism Treatment and Prevention Program at 510-231-7600.
