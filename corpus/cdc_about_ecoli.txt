Title: CDC: About E. coli

E. coli are bacteria found in intestines and the environment. Most strains are
harmless, but some cause diarrhea and serious conditions. Infection with Shiga
toxin-producing E. coli (STEC) can lead to a serious health condition called hemolytic
uremic syndrome (HUS), which risks kidney failure and death.

At-risk groups include children under 5, adults over 65, immunocompromised individuals, and
international travelers. Transmission occurs through contaminated food, water, or contact
with animals and people. Common food sources include undercooked ground beef, unpasteurized
milk or juice, and contaminated raw produce such as leafy greens, sprouts, and cucumbers.
