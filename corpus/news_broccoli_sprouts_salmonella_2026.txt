Title: Salmonella Outbreak Details - Broccoli Sprouts Recall (The Healthy)

Company: Evergreen Fresh Sprouts, LLC (Moyie Springs, Idaho)

Product: Broccoli sprouts in plastic bags or clamshell containers with expiration dates of
9/7, 9/9, 9/11, 9/14, and 9/16.

Reason for Recall: Possible Salmonella contamination; a positive Salmonella result from a
broccoli sprout sample matched the strain causing illnesses.

Case Counts: 22 total illnesses reported, including 2 hospitalizations; illness onset
occurred between July 7 and August 26, 2026.

States Affected: Idaho, Montana, Washington, and Utah (though distribution to additional
states is possible).

Bacterial Strain: Salmonella Bovismorbificans

Consumer Guidance:
- Seek medical care if experiencing symptoms (diarrhea, fever, abdominal cramps) within
  12-72 hours after eating recalled sprouts
- Clean and sanitize surfaces/containers that contacted the recalled product
- Follow FDA safe food handling protocols to prevent cross-contamination
- Report illnesses to FDA's Industry and Consumer Assistance

Note: This outbreak is separate from a concurrent alfalfa sprouts recall linked to E. coli
and Salmonella.
