Title: FDA Recall Notice: Midwest Poultry Services, L.P. Shell Eggs

Company Name: Midwest Poultry Services, L.P.
Product: White shell eggs and brown cage free shell eggs
Recall Announcement Date: July 22, 2026
Reason for Recall: Potential to be contaminated with Salmonella Enteritidis

Production Details: Eggs produced on Texas farms between June 6, 2026 and July 3, 2026, with
sell-by/best-by dates between July 20, 2026 and August 17, 2026.

Distribution: Shipped to foodservice and retail customers in Texas, Oklahoma, and Louisiana.
Available at Kroger stores (Texas and Louisiana), Brookshire Grocery stores (Texas, Oklahoma,
Arkansas, Louisiana, New Mexico, Mississippi), and other smaller retail outlets.

Quantity Recalled: 1,589,577 dozen eggs
Identifying Codes: Plant codes P-1950 or 0840962 with Julian Date between 157 and 184

Recalled Products Include:
- Kroger brand (12, 18, 30, and 60 count packages)
- Simple Truth Cage Free Brown eggs
- Brookshire's brand (6, 12, 18, 36, and 60 count)
- Country Morning brand
- Sunups brand
- Various bulk grades (AA and A)

Health Risk: Salmonella is an organism which can cause serious and sometimes fatal infections,
particularly in vulnerable populations; healthy persons may experience fever, diarrhea,
nausea, vomiting, and abdominal pain.

Consumer Guidance: Consumers who purchased products associated with this recall are urged to
avoid consumption and return the product to its original place of purchase for a full refund.
Company Contact: 574.405.9531 or recallassistance@mpseggs.com
