Title: Nut Butter Recall Update: FDA Issues Highest Risk Warning (Newsweek)

Published: Aug 27, 2026. Updated: Aug 27, 2026.

The FDA elevated a pistachio nut butter recall to Class I status on August 26, 2026 -- its
most serious risk classification. According to federal guidelines, this designation applies
when there is a "reasonable probability that use of or exposure to a product will cause
serious adverse health consequences or death."

Company: Botticelli Foods LLC
Product: Bettergoods Pistachio Nut Butter (6.7oz/190g jars)
Units Affected: 16,200 jars
Retailer: Walmart stores across 19 states

Identification Information:
- Expiration date: January 28, 2027
- UPC: 194346207961
- Lot number: LB028ACP04

Contamination Issue: The manufacturer detected Salmonella after the Florida Department of
Agriculture and Consumer Services tested three jars purchased at retail. Botticelli Foods
initiated the voluntary recall on August 3 following this notification.

Distribution States: Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Idaho, Kansas,
Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Mexico, Oregon, South Dakota, Tennessee,
Washington, and Wyoming.

Consumer Guidance: Individuals should not consume the product and should return it for a full
refund. Those experiencing Salmonella symptoms after consumption should contact a healthcare
provider. As of the announcement, no illnesses had been reported.
