Title: Investigation of Multistate Outbreak of Cyclospora Illnesses: Iceberg Lettuce (July 2026)

## Title and Status
CDC declared the outbreak concluded as of September 11, 2026, though FDA continues
investigating.

## Summary
The FDA and CDC collaborated with state and local partners to investigate a multistate
Cyclospora outbreak linked to iceberg lettuce from central Mexico. Taylor Farms de Mexico
announced that they were voluntarily removing all iceberg lettuce sourced from central Mexico
on July 17, 2026.

## Products Involved
Recalled products included Marketside-brand items sold at Walmart:
- 12-oz and 24-oz Iceberg Salad with Best if Used By dates 7/18/2026 to 8/3/2026
- 8-oz and 16-oz Shredded Lettuce with Best if Used By dates July 18th to Aug 3rd 2026
Food service customers received additional recalled products detailed in the recall notice.

## Case Counts and States
The outbreak encompassed 12,883 illnesses reported from 21 states, with 570 hospitalizations
and 2 deaths. States included: Arkansas, Georgia, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kansas, Kentucky,
Massachusetts, Maine, Michigan, Missouri, Nebraska, New Hampshire, North Carolina, Ohio,
Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, and West Virginia.

## Symptoms
Infected individuals typically developed diarrhea, with frequent bowel movements. Other common
symptoms include loss of appetite, weight loss, stomach cramps/pain, bloating, increased gas,
nausea, and fatigue.

## Consumer Recommendations
People with symptoms should contact their health care provider to report symptoms and receive
care, especially if they ate shredded iceberg lettuce in the two weeks before getting sick.
Consumers should follow FDA's safe handling and cleaning advice to reduce the risk of
cross-contamination.
