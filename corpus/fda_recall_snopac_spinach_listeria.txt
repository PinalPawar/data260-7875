Title: FDA Recall: Sno Pac Foods Frozen Spinach

Company Name: Sno Pac Foods, Inc. (Caledonia, MN)

Products Recalled:
- Del Mar 35 LB Bulk Organic Frozen Spinach
- Sno Pac 10 oz Organic Frozen Cut Spinach

Lot Codes & Expiration Dates:
- 35 LB boxes: 250107A, 250107B, 250107C, 250107D, 2501071, 2501073 (exp. 1/7/27)
- 10 oz packages: SPM1.190.5 (best by 7/9/27), SPC1.160.5 (best by 6/9/27),
  SPC2.160.5 (best by 6/9/27), SPM1.097.5 (best by 4/7/27)

Recall Reason: Potential contamination with Listeria monocytogenes
Geographic Distribution: Nationwide in retail stores and through distributors

Consumer Guidance: Dispose of the product, or return it to the place of purchase for a full
refund.

Health Risks: Infection can cause severe symptoms in vulnerable populations; miscarriages and
stillbirths among pregnant women are possible.

Status: Recall completed and FDA-terminated; no illnesses reported.
