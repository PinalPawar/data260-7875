Title: FDA Recall Notice: GreenWise Organic Frozen Berries

Company: Publix Super Market Company

Products Recalled:
- GreenWise Organic Whole Blueberries, 10-ounce, UPC 41415-06453
- GreenWise Organic Whole Blueberries, 48-ounce, UPC 41415-12053
- GreenWise Organic Whole Mixed Berries, 10-ounce, UPC 41415-06753
- GreenWise Organic Whole Mixed Berries, 48-ounce, UPC 41415-12153

Announcement Date: July 29, 2026

Reason for Recall: Potential contamination with Escherichia coli O145:H28 (E. coli O145)
which produces Shiga toxin and can cause severe stomach cramps, diarrhea (which may be
bloody), and vomiting.

States Affected: Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, North Carolina, South Carolina,
Tennessee and Virginia.

Serious Health Risks: The pathogen can cause illness characterized by severe stomach cramps
and may result in hemolytic uremic syndrome (HUS), particularly in young children, older
adults and individuals with weakened immune systems.

Consumer Guidance: Customers should not consume them, and the products should be discarded or
returned to Publix for a full refund. Consumer Contact: (800) 242-1227 from 8:30 a.m. to
5:00 p.m. Eastern time, Monday through Friday.

Status: This recall has been completed and FDA has terminated this recall.
