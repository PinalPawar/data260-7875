Title: ByHeart Infant Formula Recall - November 2025

Company: ByHeart Inc.
Product: ByHeart Whole Nutrition Infant Formula
Recall Reason: Potential Foodborne Illness - Clostridium botulinum

Affected Batch Codes:
- Batch Code 251261P2, Use by 01 Dec 2026
- Batch Code 251131P2, Use by 01 Dec 2026
UPC: 5004496800. Size: 24 oz.

Distribution: Nationwide (no specific states listed in announcement).
Risk Classification: Not specified in document.

Background: The FDA was investigating approximately 83 infant botulism cases reported since
August 2025. Of these cases, 13 infants had consumed ByHeart formula at some point. The
company stated: "The FDA has not identified a direct link between any infant formula and
these cases."

Consumer Guidance: Consumers with affected batches should immediately discontinue use and
dispose of the product. The company will replace discarded formula at no cost. Parents should
contact healthcare providers if infants show symptoms. Reporting options include calling an
FDA Consumer Complaint Coordinator, completing an electronic MedWatch form, or submitting a
paper form. Contact: 1 (833) 429-4327 (24/7), hello@byheart.com
