Title: FDA Recall Notice: TGD Cuts, LLC Cucumber Products

Company: TGD Cuts, LLC of Jessup, MD
Announcement Date: June 4, 2025
Reason for Recall: potential to be contaminated with Salmonella

Affected Products:
- Salsa, Hot 6/12 oz. (UPC: 840219170534)
- Salsa, Mild 6/12 oz. (UPC: 840219170541)
- Salsa, Mild 5 lb. (UPC: 840219140445)
- Cucumber Sliced/Grape Tomato 50/2 oz. (UPC: 840219184784)
- Cucumber Sliced Unpeeled 5 lb. (UPC: 840219160733)
- Cucumber Sliced Unpeeled 50/2 oz. (UPC: 840219170657)
- Cucumber Spears 50/2 oz. (UPC: 840219179971)

Use-By Dates: 5/19/2025 - 5/28/2025
Distribution States: Maryland, Virginia, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and North Carolina

Health Hazard: Salmonella can cause fever, diarrhea (which may be bloody), nausea, vomiting
and abdominal pain.

Consumer Guidance: If customers have product affected by this voluntary recall, they should
discard it immediately or return it to their local store for a full refund.

Status: No reported illnesses to date, and products are beyond their usable shelf life.
Contact: (410)-799-5700, M-F 6:30 a.m.-3:00 p.m. EST
