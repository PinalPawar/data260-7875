Title: FDA Outbreak Investigation: Listeria monocytogenes in Soft Cheese (June 2026)

## Overview
The FDA and CDC investigated a multi-state outbreak of Listeria monocytogenes infections
linked to requeson (soft ricotta-style cheese) manufactured by Clover Hill Dairy, LLC in
Mechanicsville, Maryland. As of August 26, 2026, the outbreak investigation concluded.

## Case Statistics
- Total Illnesses: 15
- Hospitalizations: 14
- Deaths: 1
- States Affected: Illinois, Maryland, New York, Virginia
- Illness Onset Period: March 6, 2023 to July 8, 2026

## Recalled Products & Manufacturers

Clover Hill Dairy, LLC (Primary source)
Recalled all cheese products manufactured at their facility, including soft ricotta/requeson,
cuajada, and hard cheeses in various sizes (10-14 oz clamshells to 5-gallon buckets). Products
may have been relabeled under brands including KESSO, QUESOS LA RICURA, IZALCO, DE MI PUEBLO,
and RIO LINDO.

La Ceiba Foods Latin Market Inc. (June 26, 2026 recall)
Requeson Salvadoreno and Requeson Mexicano products under La Colonia and Selectos Latinos
brands; distributed to Maryland, Virginia, and Washington D.C. from May 11 to June 1, 2026.

Nelson & Isa Lacteos, LLC (June 5, 2026 recall)
Requeson cheese sold to New York retail locations from May 15 to May 28, 2026.

## Distribution Areas
Confirmed product distribution across Maryland, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina,
Virginia, and Washington D.C., though distribution may have extended further.

## Investigation Findings
Six product samples and environmental samples tested positive for outbreak strain Listeria
monocytogenes. Of interviewed individuals, most reported consuming cheese, with several
specifically consuming Clover Hill Dairy requeson.

## Consumer/Retailer Guidance
- Do not consume recalled cheeses; check refrigerators and freezers
- Thoroughly clean and sanitize surfaces that contacted recalled products
- At-risk groups (pregnant women, persons 65+, immunocompromised individuals) should avoid
  all soft cheese varieties
- Report symptoms to healthcare providers immediately
