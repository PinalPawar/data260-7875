Title: CDC: About Botulism

Botulism is a rare but serious illness caused by a toxin that attacks the body's
nerves; it causes difficulty breathing, muscle paralysis, and potentially death. The toxin is
produced by Clostridium botulinum bacteria.

Five main types:
1. Foodborne -- from contaminated foods, especially improperly canned items
2. Wound -- when spores enter wounds; more common in people who inject drugs
3. Infant -- when spores grow in infants' intestines (often linked to contaminated formula or
   honey)
4. Iatrogenic -- from excessive toxin injection for cosmetic or medical purposes
5. Adult intestinal toxemia -- rare intestinal colonization in adults

All kinds of botulism can be fatal and are medical emergencies requiring immediate medical
attention.
