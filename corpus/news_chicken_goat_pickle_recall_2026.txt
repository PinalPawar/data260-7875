Title: Chicken Recall Summary (Newsweek)

Company: Indus Foods LLC, operating as Gangothri Foods (Austin, Texas)

Products Recalled:
- Gangothri Goat Pickle
- Gangothri Gongura Goat Pickle
- Gangothri Chicken Pickle
- Gangothri Gongura Chicken Pickle
All sold in 8-ounce glass jars.

Recall Details: Approximately 1,626 pounds affected. Production dates: January 28, 2025 -
May 6, 2026. Two-year shelf life.

Reason for Recall: The products were produced without inspection, violating federal
regulations requiring USDA inspection for meat and poultry products.

Risk Classification: Class I recall -- the highest risk category, indicating reasonable
probability that use of the product could cause serious adverse health consequences or
death.

States Affected: Nationwide distribution; specific state list not yet published by FSIS.

Consumer Guidance: Consumers should discard recalled items or return them to purchase
locations. No confirmed illnesses have been reported. Anyone experiencing illness after
consumption should contact a healthcare provider. FSIS will conduct effectiveness checks to
verify product removal from commerce.
