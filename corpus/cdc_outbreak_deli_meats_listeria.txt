Title: CDC: Listeria Outbreak Linked to Meats Sliced at Delis

This outbreak is over. 61 cases, 60 hospitalizations, 10 deaths across 19 states.
Investigation started July 19, 2024; now closed.

General guidance distinguishes healthy people (who may have mild or no symptoms) from
high-risk groups -- pregnant people, newborns, adults 65+, and people with weakened immune
systems -- who face a much higher chance of severe illness including sepsis and meningitis.
Symptoms in high-risk groups can include fever, muscle aches, and headache, sometimes with
confusion, loss of balance, or convulsions when infection spreads to the nervous system.

Additional linked pages (Updates, Locations, Timeline) provide the state-by-state case
breakdown and the specific recalled deli meat brands and lot codes over the course of the
investigation.
