Title: a2 Platinum USA Label Infant Formula Recall - May 2, 2026

Company: The a2 Milk Company ("a2MC")
Product: a2 Platinum Premium Infant Formula 0-12 months USA label
Packaging: 31.7oz tin

Affected Batches:
- Batch 2210269454, Use By 7/15/2026
- Batch 2210324609, Use By 1/21/2027
- Batch 2210321712, Use By 1/15/2027

Reason for Recall: Presence of cereulide toxin produced by some strains of the bacterium
Bacillus cereus.

Health Risks: Illness occurs through the consumption of food contaminated with the toxin and
preparing formula with hot water does not eliminate it. Symptoms include gastrointestinal
distress, typically resolving within 24 hours, though infants face increased complications
like dehydration.

Distribution: Distributed nationally through a2MC's website, Amazon and Meijer stores.
Units: 63,078 total across three batches; approximately 16,428 units sold to consumers.

Consumer Action: Consumers who have purchased the Product are recommended to discontinue use
of the Product and dispose of it immediately or return to their place of purchase for a
refund. Contact: 1-844-422-6455 (Monday-Friday, 8am-5pm Eastern).

Status: This recall has been completed and FDA has terminated this recall.
