Title: FDA Recall Notice: Nara Organics Infant Formula

Company Name: Nara Organics of New York, NY
Product: Nara Organics Whole Milk Powdered Infant Formula

Sizes and UPCs:
- 700g, UPC 860013251901
- 400g, UPC 860013251918

Recall Reason: potential risk of Clostridium botulinum contamination

Affected Lot Codes: 408125075E14F2, 708125076E14F2, 708125083E14F2, 408125139E14F2,
708125141E14F2, 708125145E14F2, 708125174E14F2, 709125273E14F2, 709125280E14F2,
709125288E14F2, 409125307E14F2, 70926019ENNB, 70926029ENNB, 70926035ENNB, 70926039ENNB,
70926042ENNB

Distribution: distributed nationally across Target retail stores, Target.com, and Nara.com
between July 2025 and June 2026.

Cases: Three hospitalized infants in California, Washington, and Pennsylvania; no deaths
reported.

Consumer Guidance: Customers should stop using the affected products immediately and contact
healthcare providers if symptoms of infant botulism appear.

Refund Process: Automatic refunds for May-June 2026 website purchases; others may request
refunds via Nara's website or return to Target.
