Title: CDC: Salmonella Outbreak Linked to Shell Eggs (closed investigation)

This outbreak is closed. Cases: 134. Hospitalizations: 34. Deaths: 0. States: 18.

The outbreak involved shell eggs produced and distributed by Midwest Poultry Services, L.P.,
which issued a recall on July 22, 2026 affecting more than 1.5 million dozen shell eggs.

CDC's standard food safety guidance for this outbreak followed the "Clean, Separate, Cook,
Chill" framework, and noted that most people infected with Salmonella experience diarrhea,
fever, and stomach cramps, usually starting 6 hours to 6 days after swallowing the bacteria.
Most people recover without treatment after 4 to 7 days. Some people, especially children
younger than 5 years, adults 65 and older, and people with weakened immune systems, may have
more severe illness that requires medical treatment or hospitalization.
