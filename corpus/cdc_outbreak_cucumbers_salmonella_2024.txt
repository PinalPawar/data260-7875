Title: CDC: Salmonella Outbreak Linked to Cucumbers

This outbreak is over. 113 cases across 23 states, 28 hospitalizations, 0 deaths. The
outbreak involved whole fresh cucumbers grown by Agrotato, S.A. de C.V. in Sonora, Mexico.

The guidance emphasized four food safety steps: Clean, Separate, Cook, and Chill, with
recommendations to wash hands, utensils, and surfaces often, and rinse fruits and vegetables
under running water before eating, cutting, or peeling.
