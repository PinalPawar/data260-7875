Title: CDC: Infant Botulism Outbreak Linked to Powdered Infant Formula (June 2026)

Product: Nara Organics Whole Milk Organic Infant Formula (recalled June 13, 2026).
Cases: 4 confirmed cases across 3 states, all hospitalized, no deaths. Status: Closed
investigation. Cause: Clostridium botulinum contamination.

Key Actions for Parents: Discard any recalled formula. Watch infants for symptoms (poor
feeding, loss of head control, difficulty swallowing) for up to a month. Seek immediate
medical care if symptoms appear.

For Healthcare Providers: Call the Infant Botulism Treatment and Prevention Program
(510-231-7600) immediately if botulism is suspected. Use BabyBIG (R) treatment without
waiting for lab confirmation. Report all suspected cases to state health departments.
